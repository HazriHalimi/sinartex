STATE-OF-THE-ART PRODUCTION FACILITY

From Fiber to Finished Industrial Textile

Precision engineering at every stage. We integrate computerized yarn warping, high-speed industrial weaving, knife-over-roll coating, thermal lamination, and high-frequency welding under disciplined quality protocols.

OUR 7-STAGE INTEGRATED PRODUCTION FLOW

Controlled Manufacturing Capabilities

Operational confidence, from first specification to ongoing supply.

STAGE 01

High-Tenacity Yarn Selection & Warping

Precision conditioning and direct electronic warping of high-tenacity filament beams.

Raw synthetic filaments and high-tenacity polyester yarns are inspected for elongation, linear density, and tensile strength before entering our computerized warping creels. Electronic tension sensors ensure perfectly balanced warp sheet density, eliminating loose ends and tension variance prior to the weaving floor.

Yarn Warping
STAGE 02

Industrial Precision Weaving

Heavy-duty rapier and air-jet looms weaving plain, panama, and ripstop constructions up to 3.2m width.

Operating advanced high-efficiency industrial looms capable of weaving up to 320 cm in width. We weave high-density base scrims, heavy ballistic canvas, panama tarpaulin structures, and specialized ripstop patterns with tight weft insertion and consistent structural stability.

Precision Weaving
STAGE 03

Advanced Knife-over-Roll Coating

Automated coating lines applying PVC plastisol, PU, and acrylic formulations with multi-zone curing.

Our knife-over-roll coating lines apply precise thicknesses of liquid polymers (PVC plastisol, polyurethane, and water-based acrylics) onto one or both sides of the woven substrate. Multi-stage heated curing ovens ensure complete gelation, polymer cross-linking, and high peeling adhesion.

Coating Lines
STAGE 04

Thermal Lamination & Multi-Layer Bonding

Hot-melt and heated calender multi-nip bonding for composite barrier textiles.

Equipped with state-of-the-art lamination machinery that bonds functional films (PVDF, PTFE, microporous PU), aluminum barrier foils, and non-woven scrims together with zero solvent emissions. Heated roller nip lines maintain uniform pressure across the entire web.

Thermal Lamination
STAGE 05

High-Frequency (HF) & Thermal Seam Welding

Specialized fabrication creating molecular-level airtight and watertight industrial seams.

High-frequency electromagnetic and hot-air welding equipment capable of fabricating wide-width tarpaulins, tensile architectural panels, and heavy-duty container liners. The molecular fusion creates seam strength equal to or greater than the original base fabric.

HF Welding
STAGE 06

Rigorous QC & In-House Laboratory Testing

On-site testing for tensile load (ASTM D5034), hydrostatic head (AATCC 127), and flame retardancy.

Every production batch undergoes comprehensive testing in our on-site physical testing laboratory. We verify warp/weft breaking strength, trapezoidal tear resistance, hydrostatic water column, adhesion peel strength, UV Xenon arc accelerated weathering, and flame retardancy compliance (DIN 4102 B1 / NFPA 701).

QC & Testing
STAGE 07

Precision Slitting, Inspection & Packaging

Continuous optical defect inspection, custom roll slitting, and heavy-duty shrink-wrapped export packaging.

Finished fabrics pass through computerized illuminated inspection tables with digital yardage counters. Rolls are slit to exact custom widths, edge-trimmed, and wound onto heavy-duty cardboard cores before being sealed in heavy moisture-proof poly-wrap and labeled with unique batch traceability codes.

Slitting & Packaging

QUALITY & TESTING COMMITMENT

Rigorous Laboratory Standards

Every textile batch undergoes automated optical defect scanning, tensile breaking load verification (ASTM D5034), tear strength validation (ASTM D2261), hydrostatic head water column testing (AATCC 127), and flame retardancy compliance. We provide official Certificate of Analysis (CoA) with commercial dispatches.
