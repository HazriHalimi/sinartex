ENVIRONMENTAL STEWARDSHIP

Sustainable Production Practices

Sinar Tex Industries integrates environmental stewardship, resource efficiency, and workplace safety across our weaving, coating, and finishing operations.

Operating a modern industrial facility in Telok Panglima Garang, Malaysia, we continually modernize our machinery and processes to minimize emissions, reduce waste, and uphold rigorous B2B manufacturing standards.
  • Energy Efficiency & Heat Recovery — Looms equipped with Variable Frequency Drives (VFD) and heat-recovery exhaust exchangers on coating lines.
  • Closed-Loop Water & Zero-Effluent Cooling — Recirculated cooling tower circuits preventing process wastewater discharge.
  • Waste Minimization & Material Circularity — 100% in-house recycling of selvage yarn clippings and thermoplastic scrap.
  • Responsible Chemistry & Low-VOC Formulations — Compliant with REACH and RoHS standards, free from heavy-metal stabilizers.
  • Workplace Safety & Employee Welfare — Stringent DOSH/OSHA protocols, comprehensive PPE, and regular operator training.

GREEN INITIATIVES

Measurable Impact & Sourcing Standards

Continuous investment in environmental responsibility throughout the yarn value chain.

Energy Optimization

Variable Frequency Drives (VFD) installed on all major looms to reduce peak power demand by up to 35% during production cycles.

Zero-Discharge Water System

Closed-loop cooling water recirculation eliminates industrial wastewater discharge, protecting local waterways in Telok Panglima Garang.

Selvage Recycling Program

All yarn edge clippings and thermoplastic coating scrap are compounded back into production inputs, achieving near-zero landfill output.

Sustainable Raw Material Sourcing

Preferred supplier audits for OEKO-TEX certified polyester and responsibly sourced cotton for blended yarn programs.

REACH & RoHS Compliance

All coating formulations are free from restricted phthalates, heavy-metal stabilizers, and substances of very high concern (SVHC).

Continuous Process Audits

Quarterly internal environmental audits and annual third-party factory assessments to maintain transparency and drive improvement.
