WHO WE ARE

About Sinar Tex Industries

A premier Malaysian manufacturer of high-performance industrial textiles, coated fabrics, and technical yarns for demanding B2B commercial applications.

Established with a focus on engineering precision, Sinar Tex Industries Sdn. Bhd. has built a reputation for reliable supply, technical consistency, and responsive customer service. Our manufacturing facility in Telok Panglima Garang, Selangor, is equipped with high-speed industrial weaving looms, knife-over-roll coating lines, thermal lamination systems, and comprehensive quality control laboratories.

Industrial Textiles &
Technical Yarns

Purpose-built industrial fabrics, tarpaulins, and high-tenacity yarns bringing dependable performance to every stage of manufacturing.

01

Heavy-Duty PVC Coated Tarpaulin

Extreme-weather waterproof tarpaulin with high-tenacity polyester scrim and dual-sided knife PVC coating, 650–900 GSM.

Architectural Tensile Membrane

Structural architectural fabric with PVDF topcoat for stadium canopies, walkway shades, and tensile structures.

03

Reinforcement Scrim & Base Cloth

Industrial Ventilation & Filtration Mesh

05

Marine Grade Protective Textile

06

Commercial Awning & WeatherShield Textile

High-Tenacity Polyester Filament Yarn

08

Engineered Synthetic Blended Yarn

Custom spun polyester-cotton and synthetic core-spun yarns for workwear, filtration, and canvas.

HOW WE MAKE IT

From Fiber to Finished Industrial Textile

01

Yarn Selection & Warping

Precision conditioning and electronic warping of high-tenacity filament beams.

02

Industrial Precision Weaving

Heavy-duty rapier looms weaving plain, panama, and ripstop constructions up to 3.2m width.

03

Knife-over-Roll Coating

Automated PVC, PU, and acrylic coating lines with multi-zone curing ovens.

04

Thermal Lamination

Hot-melt multi-nip bonding for composite barrier textiles and functional films.

05

HF & Thermal Welding

Molecular-level airtight and watertight industrial seam fabrication.
